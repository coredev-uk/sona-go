---
title: Scrobbling and Discord
description: Send what you play to Last.fm and ListenBrainz, and show it on Discord.
---

Sona can add each song you play to Last.fm or ListenBrainz, and show it as your Discord activity. All three are off until you connect them.

## Connect Last.fm

1. Open Settings → Connections.
2. Choose Connect under Last.fm.
3. Allow Sona on the Last.fm page that opens in your browser. Sona continues by itself.

## Connect ListenBrainz

1. Copy your user token from your ListenBrainz settings.
2. In Settings → Connections, paste it under ListenBrainz and connect.

## Show your song on Discord

Turn on Discord status in Settings → Connections. Sona shows the song with its artwork and time, and clears it when you pause. Discord must be running.

## How a listen counts

A song counts once you have played half of it or four minutes. Radio and songs of 30 seconds or less are not sent. Songs you play offline are sent when you are back online. Nothing is sent while [private listening](/guide/private-listening/) is on.

## Disconnect

Choose Disconnect on the service. Sona removes its sign-in and any listens it had not sent. Signing out of Apple Music does not disconnect them.

Some builds have no Last.fm key or Discord application ID. Settings → Connections says so.
