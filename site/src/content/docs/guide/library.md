title: Your Library

Sona shows your Apple Music library under Library in the sidebar. It keeps a copy on your computer, so pages open at once and work offline.

## Browse the library

The Library section lists these pages:

- **Recently added**: the newest items in your library.
- **Songs**, **Albums** and **Artists**: everything you added, with sort and filter controls at the top of each page.
- **Playlists**: your playlists and folders. The sidebar lists them too.
- **Favourite songs** and **Music videos**. Sona copies your music videos too, so the page lists them with no connection; playing one needs a connection.
- **History**: songs you played, grouped by day.

Select an item to open it. Right-click it, or press Shift+F10 or the Menu key on a focused row, for its menu: Play next, Play last, Add to playlist, Create station and more.

## Sync the library

Sona syncs after you sign in, music videos included. While it syncs, a short bar under the bell in the top bar moves, and [Notifications](/guide/notifications/) shows what Sona is syncing and how far it is. If a sync fails, the bell gets a red dot and Notifications says why, with Try again. To sync again, open Notifications and choose Sync now at its foot.

## Manage playlists

1. Press Ctrl+N, or use New playlist or folder in the sidebar.
2. Name the playlist and choose a cover.
3. Add songs from any item's menu with Add to playlist, or drag songs, albums and playlists onto a playlist in the sidebar.

In a playlist you can reorder songs by dragging them, or with Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ on a focused row.

## See your history

History lists what you played, by day. When you are signed in it includes plays from your other Apple Music devices. Plays on this computer are kept for a year. [Private listening](/guide/private-listening/) keeps plays out of it.
