title: Notifications
description: What Sona tells you about, and where to find it, behind the bell in the top bar.

Sona keeps what happens while you're not asking behind the bell in the top bar, next to your account. Nothing pops up over your desktop unless you turn that on.

## The bell

- A dot on the bell means there is something to look at. A red dot means something failed and needs you.
- While Sona is busy, such as syncing your library or downloading Widevine, a short bar under the bell moves as the work does.
- Select the bell, or Tab to it and press Enter, to open Notifications. Press Escape to close it.

## What's in it

At the top is what is going on now:

- **Library sync**: what Sona is syncing and how far it is. If a sync fails, it says why and offers Try again.
- **Add-ons**: an add-on waiting for your approval, or one that can't run. Review opens Settings → Add-ons.
- **A new version of Sona**: Install and restart where Sona updates itself, or a link to the release where your package manager does. What's new opens the release notes in a window: the changes in that version, with the same Install and restart or View release at the foot. Links in the notes open in your browser, and Tab reaches them.
- **Widevine**: the first download, or why songs can't play.

Under that is what happened:

- An album you asked to be reminded of is out. Choose Remind me on an upcoming album to get one.
- New releases from your favourite artists, when [New releases](/guide/new-releases/) is on.
- An add-on that stopped several times in a row and was switched off.

New ones are in bold with a dot. Opening Notifications marks them read. Choose one to open what it's about. Point at one, or move to it with Tab, for Remove; Clear all removes every one. They are kept for 30 days.

At the foot is when your library last synced, with Sync now.

## Desktop notifications

Releases, the reminders and new releases from your artists, can also show as desktop notifications: turn on Also show releases as desktop notifications in Settings → General → New releases. Track notifications, shown when a new song starts, are separate: they are Track notifications in Settings → General.

Signing out removes the notifications about your account. An add-on's stay.
